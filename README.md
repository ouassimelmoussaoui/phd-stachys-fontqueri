# PhD Research Digital Workspace

## Overview

This repository is dedicated to the digital organization and management of resources associated with a doctoral research project.

## Objectives

- Organize scientific resources and research documents.
- Ensure proper documentation and data management.
- Maintain a clear and structured digital workspace.
- Support research reproducibility and scientific integrity.

## Repository Structure

The repository is organized into the following directories:

- 01_Research_Documentation/ – General research documentation.
- 02_Literature/ – Bibliographic resources and references.
- 03_Data_Management/ – Data management documentation.
- 04_Analysis/ – Analysis documentation and scripts.
- 05_Reports/ – Reports and academic documents.

## Data Management

Research data will be organized, documented, backed up and preserved according to good scientific practices.

Confidential research data and sensitive information will not be published in this repository.
